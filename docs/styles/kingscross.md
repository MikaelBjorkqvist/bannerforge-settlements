# Kingscross Guild — style guide (DRAFT v1)

Human(ish), classic medieval, mercantile, practical (docs/DESIGN.md). Colour: **blue**.
Palette in code: `assets-src/structures/kingscross/palette.py` (QA rejects blocks outside it).

## Palette by role
| Role | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Foundation | stone bricks, cobblestone (mixed) |
| Lower wall | cobblestone |
| Frame (posts, beams) | dark oak logs |
| Wall infill | oak planks |
| Roof | oak stairs and slabs; dark oak for trim |
| Windows | glass panes |
| Guild accent | blue wool / blue carpet (until banners are possible) |
| Yard | grass path, oak fence and gate, farmland with hidden water, cornflowers (blue), poppies, dandelions |
| Light | lanterns (posts at the gate, inside on furniture or hanging) |

## Shapes and proportions
- Stone base 1 block, cobblestone lower wall 1 block, then timber frame.
- Steep gable roofs (45°), 1-block overhang all round; gable usually faces the street.
- Cobblestone chimney with a campfire on top for smoke.

## Growth over the levels
Level 1 modest and practical; level 2 adds height (upper floor) and more detail; level 3 adds prestige
(awning, balcony or dormer, lights, richer garden). The lot never grows.

## From the references (v1)
- **Light infill between dark timber** (classic timber frame) instead of all-wood fronts; keeps fronts readable.
- **Stone-brick trim** on roof edges and gable tops; roof surfaces in dark wood (spruce / dark oak) or oak.
- Mixed bases: cobblestone with stone-brick corners and pillars; brick or cobblestone chimneys with smoke.
- Porches on posts, trapdoor shutters, flower boxes, lantern posts and pillars, yard clutter (barrels, hay).
- Use the slope: raised entrances with outside stairs where the ground falls.
- **Guild colour without banners:** blue-and-white striped wool awnings, blue wool flags on poles (W7).
- Big buildings (Town Hall, Keep, Tavern): tower + wall walk + timber hall around a courtyard (W7).

## Do / don't
- Do: keep frames readable (posts at corners and beside doors), mix stone at the base, give every lot a yard.
- Don't: fences against walls, bare blank walls longer than 3 blocks, unlit rooms, floating decoration.
