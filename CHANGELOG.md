## [Unreleased]
### Added
- Faction names: Amberwood Order (green), Kingscross Guild (blue), Brimstone Legion (red).
  The founding form shows each faction with its colour.
- Ring a village bell to call its steward. At an unclaimed village the steward offers to found a settlement:
  pick a name and a faction banner. At your own settlement, ringing the bell opens the steward's menu.
- Steward menu showing the settlement's faction, status and prosperity.
- Commission Works: pay emeralds to build and upgrade houses, stores, tavern, walls, gates, towers and barracks
  (Basic → Improved → Advanced). Each level raises prosperity. A settlement becomes a Town at 30 prosperity
  and a City at 80. Buildings don't appear in the world yet.
